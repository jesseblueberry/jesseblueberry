### A+chiever & Time+Line

I designed, programmed, and published both apps independently.

### uQuest · Hack the Hill III

I built uQuest with **Isabel Yeboah, Ditya Gupta, and Prisha Shah**. I handled all programming, including the MapLibre map, walking navigation, Gemini-powered quests, progression, marketplace, and owl customization. We collaborated on the concept and visual direction, and my teammates created much of the artwork.

### Double Agent · uOGDC Fall Jam 2026

I handled programming and game design. **Isabel Yeboah and Ditya Gupta** created the art and assets, and **Shaurya Kalra** created the music and sound design.

## About me

I’m studying for an **Honours BSc in Computer Science (Co-op)** at the University of Ottawa. Previously, at **IRegained**, I developed three gamified therapy experiences for the MyHand System: an open-world game, a rhythm game, and a drawing game.

I work with **Swift, SwiftUI, Godot / GDScript, Java, and Python**, with a working knowledge of C#. Away from Xcode, it’s 3D printing, self-hosted servers, games, pop music, and whatever technical rabbit hole has found me this week.
